How This Prototype Works

• Detects continuous movement using accelerometer data.

• Measures movement intensity and motion continuity.

• Tracks activity duration and motion consistency.

• Generates an activity confidence score instead of exact calorie estimation.

• Designed for indoor walking, treadmill, stairs and movement-based workouts.
